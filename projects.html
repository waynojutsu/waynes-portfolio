01 / COURSEWORK / MICROSOFT EXCEL

PulaPay Fraud Dashboard

Developed and evaluated a rule-based Excel dashboard for transaction monitoring. The coursework included flagging transaction patterns, testing unseen transactions, interpreting suspicious activity and considering false positives, false negatives and scalability.

What it taught me: A reviewer needs to see why a transaction was flagged. A rule can flag a legitimate transaction too.

02 / CONCEPT PITCH / HEALTH TECHNOLOGY

THE 95 — BOKA

Proposed an offline-first clinical decision-support concept intended for rural and low-connectivity settings. The idea focuses on helping health workers follow locally validated guidance, with English/Setswana input and a carefully controlled approach to referrals.

What it taught me: An offline tool still needs reliable guidance and a clear way for health workers to decide when to refer someone.

03 / FINANCIAL INCLUSION / HACKATHON PROTOTYPE

PulaOS — Money Confidence

Explored a digital financial-inclusion experience for people managing irregular incomes. The prototype concept brings together simple budgeting, savings planning, financial confidence and scam-awareness ideas, with multilingual accessibility in mind.

What it taught me: Budgeting around irregular income starts with helping someone decide what they can set aside when they’re paid.
