What my first year is teaching me

When I started my Financial Technology degree at Botswana School of Business Sciences, I thought programming would be the part I felt most comfortable with. I was already interested in software and entrepreneurship. What took more adjustment was organising my time and explaining how I reached an answer, especially when the answer depended on both a calculation and a financial decision.

Business finance made that clear. I could recognise the formulas for annuities, bonds, net present value and internal rate of return. I had more trouble when a question moved the timing of a cash flow or asked me to explain what the result meant. Working through the steps helped me see where I was getting stuck. Sometimes I knew the formula but had misunderstood what the question was asking. Asking about that earlier made practice more useful.

I had a similar experience working on a fraud-detection dashboard in Excel. Getting the spreadsheet to flag a transaction was the first step. When I tested the rules, I had to think about what a flag would actually tell someone reviewing it. A rule can catch unusual activity and still flag a legitimate customer. If a reviewer cannot see why a transaction was flagged, the result is also harder to use. That changed what I looked for when evaluating the dashboard.

The budgeting and offline healthcare concepts raised a different set of questions. A budget tool needs to make sense for someone whose income changes each month. An offline tool needs to account for what people can do when the connection drops. Those are practical constraints that affect which features are worth building first.

I still need to start difficult topics earlier. I want to practise questions before looking at worked solutions and keep shorter notes that I can explain in my own words. I also want to record why I got an answer wrong, especially when the mistake came from interpreting the question. A useful sign of progress for me is being able to explain a decision clearly enough that someone else can question it and I can see what to improve.
