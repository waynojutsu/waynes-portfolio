Study methods I'm developing

These are the methods I use to check what I understand and plan what to practise next.

ACTIVE RECALL

Close the notes. Explain the idea.

After reading about a concept, I try explaining it without looking at my notes. If I get stuck, I work through another example.

PRACTICE

Do the calculation, then interpret it.

For finance questions, practise choosing the correct method and saying what the answer means for a real decision.

FEEDBACK LOOP

Keep an error log.

Record the reason an answer went wrong: misunderstood question, wrong formula, calculation mistake or weak interpretation.

TIME MANAGEMENT

Break work into focused blocks.

Give challenging topics short, scheduled sessions before deadlines instead of relying entirely on a final revision rush.

Useful learning tools

Course notes and lecturer guidance

My starting point for understanding the module's terminology, examples and assessment expectations.

Flashcards and question banks

For checking memory of key terms and practising the difference between similar financial concepts.

Excel and small Python exercises

For testing finance calculations, working with data and making abstract ideas more concrete.
